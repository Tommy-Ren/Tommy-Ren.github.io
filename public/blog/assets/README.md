# Blog asset attribution

The blog adapts the Midnight Bento design of [Momo Blog](https://github.com/snowhejia/momo-blog) by snowhejia. Its original MIT license is retained in [momo-license.txt](../momo-license.txt).

`cat.svg` is Momo Blog's original pixel cat logo and is provided under the upstream MIT license.

The eight WebP photos (forest, coast, road, mountain, sunlight, flowers, coffee, and city) are optimized copies of Momo Blog's demonstration photos from Unsplash. They illustrate the blog and do not represent photographs taken by Tommy Ren. Original URLs, titles, dimensions, and license information are preserved in [sources.json](sources.json). The upstream metadata does not identify photographer names; no photographer attribution has been invented. The administrator's starter import uploads these images to Supabase Storage and keeps the source links and credit on each database record.

These photographs are covered by the [Unsplash License](https://unsplash.com/license), not the code's MIT license. They must not be redistributed as a competing image collection.
